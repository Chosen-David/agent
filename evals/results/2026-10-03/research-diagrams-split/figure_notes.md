## Design

Provisional 180 × 95 mm landscape figure; 1080 × 570 SVG viewBox. Read from the two left-hand parallel paths through sum and softmax to the right-hand output. V sits beneath the output so its direct bypass has an unambiguous endpoint. Pale blue identifies logit paths, green computational combination/normalization, tan fixed values; labels and arrow topology carry meaning independently of color. No group boundaries imply hardware, deployment, privacy, or implementation structure. DejaVu Sans with 29-unit title, 24-unit node labels and 18–20-unit supporting text. Main labels are approximately 11.3 pt at the stated width. A lower margin separates the legend and evidence note from the data flow.
