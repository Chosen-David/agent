**Figure. Baseline and candidate latency by workload and timing scope.** (a) For the `end_to_end` measurements, candidate latency is 80 versus 100 ms on small (20% lower), 100 versus 120 ms on medium (16.7% lower), and 220 versus 200 ms on large (10% higher, a regression). (b) The separate `kernel_only` measurement is 10 versus 20 ms (50% lower latency, or 2× speedup within that scope). Kernel-only improvement does not establish a 2× end-to-end improvement. Bars show the values reported in `inputs/measurements.csv`; labels give latency in milliseconds. Candidate bars use hatching as well as color. The panels use different y-axis scales, both starting at zero. Percent change is 100 × (candidate / baseline − 1), rounded to one decimal where needed. Repetition counts, independent experimental units, and uncertainty estimates were not supplied; no error bars or statistical significance are inferred.
