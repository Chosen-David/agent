specifies numerical prices and unit bases but does not specify currency. The
worker correctly retained currency as unknown. During result inspection the
controller identified this mismatch; root independently confirmed that its
pre-dispatch correspondence review had falsely approved the currency detail.

The frozen rubric, input, root review and worker output remain unchanged. The
independent grader must mark this exact affected criterion ungradable because of
the material defect, not treat the worker's correct uncertainty as a failure.
A later corrected task requires new preparation and independent material review
before execution. This is not a retrospective permission to change the rubric.

The executable material gate binds a trusted review to exact materials and
requires complete criterion coverage. It does not prove arbitrary prose truth,
and a mistaken independent reviewer can authorize inconsistent materials. The
25 fixture arithmetic/topology checks did not verify currency provenance. This
negative evidence limits any claim about the coherence improvement.
