# Cross-exemplar synthesis before drafting

The full corpus is 10 distinct published works and 182 physical selected-PDF pages; three repeated Deep Sets reference pages are disclosed. Agent-research T20 corpus is separate and already complete. Detailed per-page and figure evidence stays in the three reader records.

| Exemplar | Mechanism in writing | Use in these studies | Decision and transfer boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| EX01 | Attention mechanism before benchmark | Define exact operator then distinguish approximate mask | Adopt explanation order; no borrowed BLEU or training outcomes |
| EX02 | Controlled identity shortcut contrast | Match inputs and baseline, expose weakest configuration | Adapt counterfactual; finite scheduler trace is not residual-learning evidence |
| EX03 | Geometry diagram and overlap-tile context | Specify dimensions, skip crops, and boundary conditions | Adopt geometry contract; reject trained-segmentation claims |
| EX04 | Exactness, IO, runtime and accuracy have separate evidence | Present stable merge and numerical validation separately from shape accounting | Adopt separation; reject GPU/peak-memory/speed claims |
| EX05 | Dependency topology plus observed scheduling timeline | Expose which task was served at each tick | Adapt trace view; reject throughput or starvation theorem |
| EX06 | State theorem assumptions before application breadth | Derive conditional shape recurrences then show counterexample | Adapt assumptions; reject universality from finite cases |
| EX07 | Mechanism hypothesis and optimization evidence differ | Do not recast explanatory hypothesis as proven causality | Adapt careful mechanism wording; no normalization experiment claim |
| EX08 | Train/test distinction and controlled ablations | Separate fixed-weight probe from learned performance | Adapt scope; no generalization-quality extrapolation |
| EX09 | Algorithm update plus negative-condition evidence | Show exact running state and numeric guard conditions | Adapt recurrences; no convergence guarantee borrowed |
| EX10 | Visual abstraction maps one axis to one operation | Use aligned conditions and explicit axis meaning | Adopt visual semantics; do not infer GN effects for padding |

The coherent approach is to identify a bounded scientific question, explain the mechanism and its nearest counterfactual, present a discriminating comparison, and state the supported consequence. Different studies need different structure: runtime transition semantics, numerical operator composition, and spatial geometry. Reproducibility commands and audit ledgers belong in companions/appendices, while assumptions essential to interpreting a result stay beside that result. Negative evidence should constrain the conclusion without replacing the argument with repeated caveats.

This is adaptation of existing workflow, not evidence of improved model capability. A baseline full-draft test precedes any writing Skill edit; if it succeeds, do not manufacture a prompt change. The third manuscript is held out from intervention design. The 8 fixed acceptance criteria are not given as grading answers to writers.
