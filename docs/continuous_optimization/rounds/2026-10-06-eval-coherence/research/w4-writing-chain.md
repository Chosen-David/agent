## Minimum candidate, not yet implemented

Retain current skill, validators, roles and evidence. Pilot one small `scientific_brief` supplied with the writer's ordinary material, derived entirely from that same frozen source pack:

1. Reader's research question and setting; closest supported alternative and its relevant limitation (or explicit unknown).
2. Central answer/contribution, mechanism and material design choice; distinguish established method from this study's contribution.
3. Decisive evidence with exact source position, metric definition, allowed interpretation and scope; retain null/adverse/conflicting evidence and missing facts.
4. Section/figure purpose: which question it answers, which comparison or mechanism the reader should see, and what the result does **not** establish.

This is a bounded rendering of the existing ledger/blueprint. Provenance IDs belong in companion evidence unless they help the paper's scientific explanation. Paragraphs should develop claim/reason/evidence/implication when appropriate; do not impose that sequence mechanically on every paragraph. Prefer concrete mechanisms and comparisons over generic novelty language. Do not add a novelty claim, invented baseline, numerical result or causal conclusion to make the prose more assertive.

Candidate integration point, **only after observed improvement**: paper_writing_workflow §4.1/Phase B, with a concise reference in execution.md if needed; sync generated plugin references using the existing script. No proposed acceptance-schema expansion. Existing delivery contract already owns the distinction this candidate tries to make easier to execute.
