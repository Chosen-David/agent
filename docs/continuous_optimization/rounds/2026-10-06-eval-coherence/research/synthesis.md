## Integrated decisions

1. **Execute the existing design before altering it.** diagram Skill already requires source nodes/edges, final-render checks, final-size/full-page QA and separate scientific/design acceptance. Source audit confirms actual upstream marker checker only validates technical marker references. This supports real three-paper evaluation, not a longer prompt or another role. Retain unchanged Skill unless an observed failure plus a discriminating repair experiment warrants a reusable change.
2. **Separate evidence layers and preserve every failure.** Source/code reader hands off method facts; renderer produces editable geometry and exported pixels; independent reviewer traces critical connections and reads at final size; writer explains mechanism and limitation; final reader examines the embedded result. Published figure is a design comparator, not ground truth for all permissible layouts. Both scientifically-correct-but-ugly and attractive-but-wrong outputs must remain visible.
3. **Keep maintext scientific and audit evidence separate.** For T24 later full-draft tests, test a small scientific brief before adding more workflow prose. Provenance records stay in companion evidence; prose explains research question, mechanism, decisive evidence and scope. Current T25 figure/caption integration is not three full manuscripts and cannot close T24.

Adopted here only as evaluation design: DG-02 separate gates and source-grounded actual artifacts. Pending local trials: DG-01, DG-03, WE-PR/DP/PB/SQ-01; their minimal experiments/costs and concrete file mappings are in reader notes. Rejected: wholesale runtimes, copied long prompts/role quotas, aesthetic aggregate as correctness, same-section figure score shortcut, inferred graph as oracle, numerical provenance as interpretation proof, and absence of reviewer findings as proof of no errors. No new paid service/GPU or model-weight change.
