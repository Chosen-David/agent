# Fifty-round token optimization

The 2026-10-12 request is tracked in [TOK-FIFTY](../../agent_doc/task/task_details/TOK-FIFTY.md). Each successful count needs a distinct reachable improvement, frozen measured token reduction, independent quality/result review and verified ordinary main publication. Previous mechanisms and repeated benchmark cases do not count again.

Focus on long-document and accumulated advice navigation, then dependency-aware multi-role packets and runtime hotspots. Retain raw history and cancelled/blocked work. Navigation or an empty change list cannot certify meeting closure or release a scientific consumer.

Report supplied input, output, total and paid experiment spend separately. Whole development/review, recovery calls, cache pricing and cash cost remain separate and unknown until actually measured. Short fixture gains do not establish long-horizon savings. Goal telemetry is a host counter; its aggregation and billing coverage are not independently verified.

Research leads: [AgentFold](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.24699), [ACM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23809) and [Context as a Tool](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.22087). Their abstracts motivate retrievable context editing; local acceptance remains mandatory and paper metrics are not local measurements.
