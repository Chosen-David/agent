## Independent small check and limits

For l=1 m and g=9.81 m/s², sqrt(l/g)=0.3192754284070505 s; converting to l=100 cm and g=981 cm/s² gives exactly the same computed value. This validates only the unit conversion in this example, not the physical law or C. Failure case: l/g has units of time squared, not period; omitted damping or finite amplitude can invalidate a model even when dimensions match. Positive lengths/gravity and a complete variable list are prerequisites; the completeness of any real apparatus model remains unknown. No formal proof or empirical oscillator validation was performed.
