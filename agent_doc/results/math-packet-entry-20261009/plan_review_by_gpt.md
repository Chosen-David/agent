## Mathematical and causal scope assessed

For Q_n<=x<Q_{n+1}, the floor packetizer gives `0 <= x-P(x) < l_{n+1} <= L`; the completed backlog differs by exactly that residue. `P(x)>=Q_n iff x>=Q_n` gives equality of complete-boundary hit sets, including empty sets with delay infinity. It does not equate arbitrary partial-work thresholds or out-of-order task IDs.

The service weakening follows only in the stated packetized-input terminal model: P is monotone and right-continuous, so for finite nonnegative infimum `P(inf f)=inf P(f)`; for boundary A(s), `P(A(s)+y)>=A(s)+[y-L]_+`. The rate-latency shift is a weaker service guarantee, not an assertion of actual added terminal delay.
