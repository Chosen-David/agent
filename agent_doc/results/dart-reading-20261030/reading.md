## Method and assumptions

The recovery object is (skeleton, concrete entity, occurrence ordinal), not merely a reusable stage. Failure must already be observable at an action boundary; no silent-error detection or general root-cause discovery. Reviewed configurations supply instance resolvers, entry states, commit/exit predicates, declared input/output keys and effect policies. DART does not automatically discover their semantic completeness.

Four steps (§4): uniquely identify the failed instance or abstain; certify reviewed boundaries as decidable/closed/separable/controllable; materialize instance-bound entry/commit/optional-exit checkpoints; select the most recent admissible checkpoint after dependency and effect vetoes. Default restore searches entry/commit, not every recorded exit. Step read/write sets are conservative annotations from manifests/planner maps/tool contracts; unioned instance sets induce edges by write/read intersection (I.1 Eq12–14). Incomplete annotations can invalidate safety; conservative over-approximation can block safe work.

Two obligations differ: a closed handoff at checkpoint creation does not establish that rollback remains admissible after a downstream consumer commits. The sidecar registry and named snapshots retain different information; Registry-Only reconstructs snapshot-manager bookkeeping at restore time. This is not a generic checkpoint-file format patch.

Appendix H's theorem concerns rollback that leaves a dependent consumer committed **without compensation, invalidation or joint rollback**. It does not ban those alternatives. Lemma1 largely unfolds the stated conjunction; latest-checkpoint selection requires a maximum to exist, not only total ordering (finite materialized checkpoints suffice). The acquired HTML references A2/A3/A4 but contains no separately labeled definitions located by a full-text check; their intended roles are partly described inline. Treat the proof presentation as scoped/underspecified, not a mechanically verified universal theorem. PDF correspondence remains unchecked.
