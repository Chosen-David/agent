The new card fills a real and specific boundary: individual fixed-matrix stability does not certify arbitrary switching. Common SPD contraction and per-mode metric conversion are appropriate elementary sufficient certificates. The four-node causal order is correct and avoids combining producer output with downstream conclusions before distinct independent validation. Exact rational finite examples are a suitable validation aid, provided the general bounds are separately proved. No GPU, model, token saving or production claim is proposed.

The prior result search occurred before data production, records partial coverage and missing records, and does not claim matched reusable science. Related MATH-61 knowledge has pinned metadata and the current task explicitly limits it to design reference. Irrelevant search hits should remain uninterpreted; they do not invalidate the new topic or justify a universal no-prior-result claim. GUIDE.md is empty, so no human-authored substantive instruction can be inferred from its existence.

Two issues block approval. First, the current plan does not specify the exact mathematical conventions that distinguish a valid switching bound from an off-by-one or squared-factor error. The shared metric must be SPD and uniform, the origin common, the quantity contracted and the switch count explicit, and the dwell threshold strictly below one. Finite mode stability and per-mode Lyapunov solutions alone do not prove switched stability. The boundary at equality must not be promoted to decay.

Second, the immutable acceptance object remains underdetermined. Six generic pass statements are not sufficient to freeze a scientific and retrieval acceptance contract. The verifier needs exact required example coverage, query/rank criteria, context prerequisite closure, preservation scope, reproducibility/regression checks, output ownership and producer/consumer completion predicates before raw data exist. This revision is an operational clarification of the authorized card, not a request for larger experiments or another scientific direction.

The 1800-second/two-cycle budget is adequate for this narrow plan if both blocking changes are made together. Consume this as cycle 1 and do not reset started_unix or the logical budget. Actual model cost and tokens remain unknown. This review does not replace the later distinct fresh result verifier's inspection of code, proof, sources, raw data, query behavior and hashes.
